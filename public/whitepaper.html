검증 가능한 연결

IEUM은 일상 서비스와 디지털 자산을 연결하기 위해 Rust로 구축한 공개 원장입니다. PoS 참여와 BFT 최종성, Ethereum 계열 주소·JSON-RPC 호환성, QUIC 기반 P2P, 독립 노드 검증과 공개 Explorer를 하나의 운영 가능한 체계로 묶습니다.

이 문서는 기술 설명이며 투자 권유, 가격 보장 또는 수익 약속이 아닙니다. 모든 수치와 계획은 공개 소스 및 실제 네트워크 상태로 독립 검증해야 합니다.

WHY IEUM

문제와 접근법

실사용 연결

원장만 만드는 대신 Wallet, 오프라인 Cold Wallet, Explorer/Manager와 AAH 서비스 접점을 함께 제공합니다.

확정성 중심

검증자가 proposal, prevote, precommit 단계로 합의하고 2/3 초과 투표권의 인증서를 통해 확정 상태를 증명합니다.

검증 가능한 운영

체인 ID, genesis hash, 노드 버전, 검증자 서명률, 공급량과 거래를 읽기 전용 Explorer에서 공개합니다.

ARCHITECTURE

기술 설계

01

합의와 최종성

알려진 가중 검증자 집합이 BFT 라운드에 참여합니다. 정상 네트워크에서 1/3 미만의 악의적·장애 투표권을 가정하며, 확정 블록은 단순히 가장 긴 체인을 기다리는 방식과 다릅니다.

02

조정 가능한 블록 간격

코드는 최소 목표 간격을 100ms~15초 범위에서 허용하고 기본값은 5초입니다. 운영 권장 프로파일은 100ms~5초이며 실제 확정 시간은 검증자 수, 합의 타임아웃, 지연과 부하에 좌우됩니다. 100ms는 처리량 또는 최종성 보장이 아닙니다.

03

네트워크와 동기화

libp2p/QUIC P2P, 영구 Peer ID, 직접 블록 동기화와 2/3 인증 snapshot/checkpoint를 결합합니다. 동기화 후보는 독립 피어로 교차 확인합니다.

04

사용자 호환 계층

0x 계정, secp256k1 서명, 18자리 단위와 Ethereum 계열 JSON-RPC를 지원해 기존 지갑 경험과 도구 연동의 진입 장벽을 낮춥니다. EVM 전체 호환을 의미하지는 않습니다.

More than fast records:
verifiable connections

IEUM is a public ledger built in Rust to connect everyday services with digital assets. It brings together PoS participation, BFT finality, Ethereum-style addresses and JSON-RPC, QUIC-based P2P networking, independent-node verification, and a public explorer in one operable system.

WHY IEUM

Problem and approach

Connected utility

IEUM combines the ledger with a Wallet, offline Cold Wallet, Explorer/Manager, and touchpoints across AAH services.

Finality first

Validators proceed through proposal, prevote, and precommit, producing a certificate backed by more than two-thirds of voting power.

Observable operations

Chain ID, genesis hash, node versions, signing rates, supply, and transactions are exposed through a read-only explorer.

ARCHITECTURE

Technical design

01

Consensus and finality

A known, weighted validator set participates in BFT rounds. The model assumes less than one-third faulty or malicious voting power under normal network conditions and does not rely on waiting for a probabilistic longest chain.

02

Adjustable block interval

The implementation accepts a minimum target interval from 100 ms to 15 s and defaults to 5 s. The operating profile presented here is 100 ms to 5 s. Actual finality depends on validator count, consensus timeouts, latency, and load; 100 ms is not a throughput or finality guarantee.

03

Network and synchronization

libp2p/QUIC, persistent Peer IDs, direct block sync, and snapshots/checkpoints certified by two-thirds are combined with independent-peer cross-checks.

04

User compatibility layer

0x accounts, secp256k1 signatures, 18-decimal units, and Ethereum-style JSON-RPC lower migration friction. This does not claim full EVM compatibility.

DESIGN REFERENCES

Lessons from established systems

ETHEREUM / GETH ECOSYSTEM

Familiar accounts and RPC

IEUM adopts familiar address, signature, and JSON-RPC conventions. It does not claim Ethereum's security scale or EVM equivalence.

SOLANA

Latency-aware experience

IEUM values responsive user experience while reporting target interval separately from observed finality. No unverified TPS claim is made.

COSMOS / COMETBFT

Explicit BFT voting

The design draws on the proven proposal, prevote, precommit, and greater-than-two-thirds commit pattern. IEUM is an independent implementation, not a claim of Cosmos SDK or IBC support.

TOKEN ECONOMICS

Economic and participation principles

Role of IEUM

  • Value transfer and network fees
  • PoS validator participation and consensus rewards
  • Connections to AAH services and community experiences
  • Publicly verifiable ownership and movement records

Supply transparency

The explorer reads total issued, circulating, and locked balances from chain RPC. Maximum supply, treasury allocation, lockups, and rewards must follow versioned on-chain configuration and public policy.

Price appreciation, principal, liquidity, and continuing rewards are not guaranteed.

SECURITY MODEL

Security is an operating rule

Key separation

Validator, node identity, and user keys are separated; Cold Wallet supports review and signing away from the network.

Multiple checks

Independent RPC quorum, BFT certificates, snapshot signatures, and genesis/version checks avoid trusting one node response.

Least privilege

The public Manager is read-only. Administration and recovery require separate authentication and auditability.

Current risks: concentrated early validator membership, limited production history, need for external security audits, and market, regulatory, and liquidity uncertainty. Open source enables review; it does not prove defect-free software.
